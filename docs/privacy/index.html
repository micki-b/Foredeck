FOREDECK PRIVACY POLICY

Your crew.
Your conversation.

This policy describes the Foredeck iPhone app and this website, maintained by micki-b.

Effective November 19, 2026

The essentials

Foredeck has no developer-operated account service, advertising SDK, or third-party analytics SDK. Crew audio and shared position information travel between participating phones. Apple services, an optional Signal K server, and GitHub have their own data handling described below.

Information used by the app

Voice and microphone

With your permission, Foredeck captures microphone audio for the crew intercom. Audio is processed and buffered for live transmission and playback. The app does not provide a voice-recording feature or save conversations as audio files, and it does not send voice to a developer-operated server. Other participants can hear transmitted audio and may independently record it outside Foredeck.

Crew identity and pairing

The app stores your chosen display name, station, settings and an app-generated device identifier locally. Its initial display name may come from your iPhone’s name; you can change it under Settings. Your boat name is saved in local preferences and the pairing code is saved in the iOS Keychain.

Display name, station, identifiers and speaking status are used for discovery and crew coordination. Nearby discovery can reveal a display name, station and technical discovery identifiers to nearby devices. During a session, participating crew receive identity and shared session information. Do not use a display name you would not want others nearby to see.

Location and anchor information

With your permission, Foredeck uses your location, accuracy and related readings for boat position, the recent track and anchor features. While a crew session is active, it shares the effective boat or phone position and associated accuracy/time information with participating phones. Anchor drop points, docking targets, rode length, alarm settings and alarm events are also shared.

Background location access may be used to support anchor monitoring. The current app keeps its recent track and shared anchor state in memory rather than offering a developer-hosted location history.

Camera and notifications

Camera access is used to scan invitation QR codes. Foredeck does not save or upload camera images. Notifications support anchor alerts and may show alarm information on your lock screen depending on your iPhone settings.

Connections and other services

Crew connections: Foredeck uses Apple’s Multipeer Connectivity with encryption required for session traffic. A pairing code is an access credential; keep it private. Encryption does not stop a participating crew member from retaining information they receive.

Apple Maps and iOS: Map content and location services are provided through Apple frameworks. Apple may process map requests and related technical or location information under its own policies. Apple also controls App Store transactions and any diagnostics you choose to share. See Apple Maps & Privacy and Apple’s privacy information.

Signal K: If you enable this optional connection, Foredeck connects to the server host and port you enter and requests boat GPS and instrument readings. The server operator may receive connection information and keep its own logs. The current connection uses unencrypted WebSocket transport; use a trusted network. Boat position obtained from that server can be shared with connected crew in place of the phone position.

Storage and retention

Local preferences and pairing information persist so you can rejoin your boat. Recent track, session state and live audio buffers are held in memory; their lifetime depends on the session and app process. Foredeck does not maintain a developer-hosted archive of conversations or location tracks.

iOS controls device backup behavior. Preferences may be included in device backups. The app marks its pairing-code Keychain entry as device-only. Keychain data can outlast app removal, so unpair before uninstalling if you want the saved pairing code removed. Other participants’ copies and third-party service logs are outside the app’s control.

Your choices and deletion

  • Choose a display name and station under Foredeck Settings.
  • Tap Leave on the Crew screen to end your active session.
  • Use Settings → Unpair this boat to end the session and remove this phone’s saved boat name and pairing code.
  • Revoke microphone, location, camera, local network or notification access in iPhone Settings. Related features may stop working.
  • Turn off Signal K under Boat instruments to disconnect from that server.
  • Removing the app removes its app-container data, subject to iOS backup/restore behavior; it may not remove Keychain entries. There is no developer-hosted app account to delete.

To ask about privacy or information you voluntarily supplied through support, use the support contact channel. Do not post sensitive data publicly; ask for an appropriate private channel if needed. We can only act on information under the maintainer’s control.

This website and support requests

This website adds no analytics, advertising trackers, tracking cookies, embedded third-party media or externally hosted fonts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and may process other technical information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you contact support through GitHub, your GitHub identity, request, attachments and replies can be public. The maintainer uses information you choose to submit to answer questions, investigate problems and improve Foredeck. GitHub controls hosting, retention and account data for those requests. Do not submit pairing codes, precise locations, credentials or other sensitive information. Contact the maintainer through support if you need help with information you previously posted.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when Foredeck’s data handling changes. The effective date above identifies this version. Questions can be directed to Foredeck support.

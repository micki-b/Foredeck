How do I pair the crew?

On the first phone, enter a boat name and tap Create. Open Invite crew to show the QR code. On each other phone, choose Scan the QR code instead, or enter the boat name and code manually. The boat name must match exactly.

Keep the invitation private. Anyone with the boat name and code may be able to pair.

Why can’t I find the other phones?

Keep phones nearby, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth enabled. In iPhone Settings, allow Foredeck access to the local network. Each phone must be paired to the same boat and join the crew session. Check that no one has denied the permission prompt.

A boat router is optional. If discovery fails, leave the session, reopen Foredeck and join again. Test direct connectivity on your own devices before relying on it on the water.

Why can’t the crew hear me?

Allow microphone access for Foredeck in iPhone Settings. Join the crew session and check your talk mode. Open mic transmits when speech opens the voice gate; Push to talk requires holding the large talk control.

Check the iPhone audio output, headset connection, playback volume and any per-crew mute settings. In wind, try a headset and adjust microphone sensitivity under Settings → Voice.

How do I set up the anchor view?

Allow location access, join a session, and open Anchor. Once a position is available, tap Drop anchor here. Set Rode out and Alarm radius, then enable Anchor alarm if wanted. Crew share anchor changes while connected.

Allow notifications and the location permissions needed for background operation. Test alarm behavior with your devices, notification settings and locked screens. Foredeck does not have Apple’s critical-alert entitlement, so do not assume an alarm will bypass every sound or Focus setting.

Do I need Signal K?

No. Without it, Foredeck uses your iPhone’s position. If you have a compatible Signal K server, enable Use Signal K under Settings → Boat instruments and enter its reachable host and port. Available GPS, depth, apparent wind and speed readings can then appear.

The current Signal K connection uses an unencrypted WebSocket. Use it only on a trusted network. Read the privacy policy for more details.

Where is night mode?

Open Settings → Display and turn on Night mode. You can also choose the night app icon there. Distance and depth units can be changed in Settings → Units.

How do I stop sharing or unpair?

Tap Leave on the Crew screen to end the session on your phone. To remove this boat’s saved name and pairing code, open Settings → Unpair this boat and confirm. You will need the invitation again to rejoin.

Unpairing does not erase copies already held on other crew members’ phones. See Your choices and deletion for more information.

How many phones can join?

A session supports up to eight devices, including your own. Each crew member needs a compatible iPhone with Foredeck. Radio range and audio quality depend on the devices and conditions; there is no guaranteed operating range.

CONTACT THE MAINTAINER

Tell us what happened.

Foredeck is maintained by micki-b. GitHub support requests are the current contact channel for app questions, problems and feedback.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Foredeck version, what you expected and what happened. Describe the steps that reproduce the problem.

Open a support request

Keep private details private.

Support requests are public. Do not include boat codes, invitation QR codes, precise locations, passwords or personal contact details. Redact screenshots before attaching them. For a privacy request, describe the request without personal data and ask the maintainer to arrange an appropriate private channel if needed.

For security vulnerabilities, use private vulnerability reporting.
